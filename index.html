Micle

🎙️ a daily microphone guessing game

Guess the studio microphone in 6 tries. Each guess is compared against the answer across six categories. In Random Mic mode, toggle Infinity to drop the guess limit entirely.

  • Exact match
  • Partial: some polar patterns overlap
  • No match
  • Answer is higher (year or price)
  • Answer is lower
  • Price unknown for this mic

Long values are shortened to fit: LDC and SDC are large- and small-diaphragm condensers, and Omni, Fig-8, Super, Hyper and Wide are the matching polar patterns. Hover a tile for the full text.

Keyboard shortcuts:

  • / Focus the guess input
  • ? Toggle these instructions
  • Esc Close these instructions
  • S Open stats
  • D Daily Puzzle
  • M Random Mic
  • N New Mic (Random Mic)
  • R Random guess
  • I Toggle Infinity (Random Mic)

Practice mode offers unlimited replays that don't count toward your streak or stats.

    Statistics

    Mic pool sourced from the Berklee College of Music microphone inventory. Price data is sourced from MSRP, the Reverb Price Guide, and in some cases with rare mics, educated guesstimations.
